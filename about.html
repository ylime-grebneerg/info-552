About Bostons

Nicknamed "The American Gentleman," the Boston Terrier was accepted by the American Kennel Club in 1893. But is this dapper gentleman right for your home? Learn more about the breed, its history, and its typical characteristics at the following sites.

The American Kennel Club provides information about the breed standard, as well as rankings for where the breed falls on various scales in regard to physical and social needs, personality traits, and suitability for family life.

The UK equivalent, the Royal Kennel Club, provides similar information to the AKC, along with details on breeding and some suggested resources for health.

The Boston Terrier Club of America is the official national breed club, and their FAQ has some great starter info.
