Boston Terrier Healthcare

One of your top priorities as a dog owner is the health of your pet! As a brachycephalic breed, Bostons have some particular healthcare needs, alongside other breed-specific concerns. We've compiled some resources here to help you be proactive about your dog's health, though you should always check in with a licensed veterinarian.

The Boston Terrier Club of America has a dedicated health committee, and provides information here on their activities, as well as links to more health information specific to the breed.

This guide from a New Jersey animal hospital lists genetic predispositions for the breed and tips for what to watch out for, preventive care, and when to call your vet.

PetMD provides a similar list of health concerns to be on the lookout for, as well as detailed information on nutrition and feeding, plus bonus training and grooming tips.
