Boston Terrier Breeders

If you're interesting in purchasing a Boston from a breeder, start here! We've compiled some resources to help you find reputable and safe breeders, but please do your own research before committing to buying to make sure you're getting a healthy dog and not getting scammed.

The Boston Terrier Club of America has loads of information about what makes a reputable breeder, in addition to search tools to get you started, and contact info for an individual who can help you find a breeder if you get stuck or are feeling overwhelmed.

The AKC Marketplace is a search tool for finding puppies for sale, allowing you to filter by location. You can see information about the breeder and their status with the AKC, as well as what kind of health testing they've performed and what documentation they provide with their puppies.

Another anecdotal source, but this Reddit post contains a wealth of information on finding a good breeder. It includes details on recommended health testing for Bostons, warning signs of irresponsible breeders, and more.
