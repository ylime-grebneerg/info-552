Getting a Boston Terrier

If you've decided a Boston Terrier is right for you after all, we've compiled some resources to start your search. Read on for some information about choosing a breeder or a rescue.

If you're not sure yet or just want to consider other options, check out the "Alternative Breeds" page to explore some other dogs!

Choosing to purchase a dog from a breeder or adopt from a shelter is often framed as an ethical decision, but it's ultimately about finding the right dog for your life from a source you can trust.

One professional trainer offers some input and personal experience on his blog, coming down in favor of responsible rescues.

While it's not from an official professional website, this blog post by a Tumblr user with a previous career in dog training provides valuable information on some red flags to watch out for in listings from shelters and rescues specifically.

This article from the website for a remote dog monitoring app offers a balanced perspective with some consdierations for why a breeder or a shelter may be the right choice for you personally.
