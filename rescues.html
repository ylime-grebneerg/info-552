Boston Terrier Rescues

If you're interesting in adopting a Boston from a rescue, start here! We've compiled some resources to help you find reputable and safe shelters, but please do your own research before committing to adopting to make sure you're getting a healthy dog that fits your lifestyle and not getting scammed.

You're not too likely to find a purebred Boston there, but it's always worth checking out your local Humane Society, as a branch of a well-regarded national organization.

The Boston Terrier Club of America has a rescue committee, which coordinates with local organizations and adopters all over the country. The site provides contact info for reaching out if you're interested in adopting a rescue.

Some additional well-regarded regional rescue organizations:

  • Boston Buddies in California
  • Boston Terrier Rescue of Florida
  • Boston Terrier Rescue of North Texas
  • SNORT (Short Noses Only Rescue Team) in the Northeast
